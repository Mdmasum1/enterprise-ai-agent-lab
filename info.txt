***How much RAM do you have? The 1B model needs roughly 2–3 GB free RAM minimum. You can check with:
   Get-CimInstance Win32_OperatingSystem | Select-Object FreePhysicalMemory
   
   Example:
    The result 655460 is in kilobytes (KB) means:
    655,460 KB ÷ 1024 = ~640 MB free RAM
